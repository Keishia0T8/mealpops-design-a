ABOUT MEAL POPS

More meals out. More love for local restaurants.

Meal POPs is a technology company that encourages people to enjoy their favorite restaurants more often, by letting restaurants offer simple, standardized monthly cash voucher promotions.

Created with the modern restaurateur in mind. We aim to deliver:

01New customers
02Loyal customers
03Profitable transactions
04Ownership of the customer experience
OUR STORY

It started with a mission bigger than lunch.

Meal POPs began as a simple way for former at risk youth in our community to raise money for advanced education, including trade school and community college.

2018

By the end of the year, we had supported educational expenses for two students.

2019

As Official Fundraising Sponsor for the Music City Icons professional basketball team, we sent student athletes to one of Nashville's best summer basketball camps.

Today

Support from premier local and national restaurant brands grew into partnerships with large membership organizations, and the restaurant cash app you know now.
