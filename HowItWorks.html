Quick answers

Is there a minimum spend?

Never. Vouchers work like cash toward anything on the menu.

Can my friends use it too?

Yes. Vouchers are per person, so each subscriber in your group uses their own.

Does it work for takeout?

Yes, for dine in and to go orders, unless a restaurant chooses otherwise.

What does it cost restaurants?

Nothing. The merchant fee per voucher redeemed is $0.00.
